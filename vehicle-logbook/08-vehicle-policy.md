### 2. Place of business

The Company's registered office and principal place of business is located at
**______________________________** (the "Base"). The Company's operations are
directed from this location, client records are maintained there, and it is the
point of departure for work-related travel.

Accordingly, travel between the Base and a client site, prospective client site,
supplier, or other business destination constitutes business travel.

### 3. Authorised use

The Vehicle is provided for the business purposes of the Company, including
travel to and from job sites, client meetings, site measurements and estimates
(whether or not the work is subsequently awarded), supplier visits, and the
transport of tools, materials and personnel.

### 5. Personal use of the Vehicle

Personal use of the Vehicle is to be kept to a minimum and must be recorded in
the logbook as personal. Personal use is not prohibited, but it is not to be
recorded as business use under any circumstances.

Where an employee other than the officer operates the Vehicle, travel between
that employee's residence and the Base is personal use (commuting) and shall be
recorded as such. Where practicable, the Vehicle shall be kept at the Base
overnight.

### 6. Logbook

A contemporaneous logbook is maintained for the Vehicle recording, for every trip:
the **date**, the **destination**, the **purpose**, and the **kilometres driven**.
Odometer readings are recorded and photographed at the beginning and end of each
taxation year.

Each operator of the Vehicle is responsible for classifying their own trips as
business or personal within a reasonable time, and for providing the purpose of
each business trip in specific terms.

### 7. Operating costs

Fuel and operating costs are normally paid by the Company. Where an operator pays
an operating cost directly to a third party, a receipt must be obtained and
submitted regardless of the method of payment, including cash.

Amounts payable by the officer to the Company in respect of personal operating
costs shall be settled no later than **45 days after the end of the Company's
taxation year**.

### 8. Records

Logbook records, odometer photographs, receipts and this policy are retained for
not less than **seven years**. The base-year logbook is retained for six years
following the last taxation year in which it is used as a base year.
